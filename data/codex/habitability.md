# Habitability

Some worlds are easier to live on than others, and a colony grows at the speed its world allows: continental worlds {hab_continental}, ocean worlds {hab_ocean}, arid worlds {hab_arid}. Ice, barren and toxic worlds are dome worlds: they grow at {hab_ice} and have half the district slots, and a Colony Ship can settle them only once you know [[tech:habitat_domes|Habitat Domes]]; the first [[building:habitat_dome|Habitat Dome]] comes with the colony. Gas giants and asteroid belts hold only an [[mechanic:outposts|outpost]].
