# Stability

Stability runs from 0 to 100 and is worked out afresh every turn, so its breakdown is the whole story. It starts at {stability_base}. Clerks, buildings, ordinances, technologies and events raise it; unemployment ({unemployed} for each thousand), homelessness ({homeless} for each thousand), an [[mechanic:shortage|energy shortage]] ({energy_short}) and [[mechanic:famine|famine]] ({famine}) lower it.

At {stable_at} or more a colony works {stable_bonus} harder; at {unstable_at} or less it loses {unstable_loss} of its output. At {unrest_at} or less there is unrest. A colony that stays at {autonomy_at} or less for {autonomy_warning} turns declares autonomy and leaves your control.
