# The market

A [[building:market_exchange|Market Exchange]] opens the market. Energy is the currency: food and minerals cost {food_buy} each to buy and fetch {food_sell} when sold; metals cost {alloys_buy} and fetch {alloys_sell}. Trades happen at once, in lots of {market_lot}, up to {market_max_lots} lots at a time. Influence and research cannot be traded.
