# Adjacency

Some districts work better beside others. Each Agriculture district next to another Agriculture district gains {adj_agriculture_agriculture}, up to {adj_agriculture_agriculture_cap}. Industry gains {adj_industry_mining} for each neighbouring Mining district, up to {adj_industry_mining_cap}, but each neighbouring Habitation district costs {adj_industry_habitation_stability} stability. Research gains {adj_research_energy} for each neighbouring Energy district, up to {adj_research_energy_cap}. The planner shows the bonus before you place a district.
