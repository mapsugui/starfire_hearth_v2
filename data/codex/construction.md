# Construction

Each colony builds one thing at a time, from a queue of up to {max_queue}. Districts go on the colony's hex slots; buildings take a building slot, {building_cap} per colony and {city_extra_buildings} more in a City. Cancelling returns the whole cost. You can [[mechanic:rush|rush]] the build in progress, neighbouring districts can help each other ([[mechanic:adjacency|adjacency]]), and technology unlocks higher district [[mechanic:tiers|tiers]]. A [[mechanic:governors|governor]] can run the queue for you.
