# Surveys and outposts

A Survey Probe needs {survey_turns} turns to survey a planet. A Construction Ship can then build an outpost there in {outpost_turns} turns for {outpost_influence} [[resource:influence|influence]]; discounts never take more than {outpost_floor} off. An outpost on an asteroid belt yields {outpost_minerals} of minerals a turn; one over a gas giant yields {outpost_energy} of energy or {outpost_research} research, whichever you choose. Each costs {outpost_upkeep} of energy upkeep.
