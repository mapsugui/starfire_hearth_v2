# Research

Research comes in three branches: physics, society and engineering. Each branch offers a hand of {hand_size} technology cards. Pick one, and that branch's research goes into it every turn. A card costs {tier_1_cost}, {tier_2_cost}, {tier_3_cost} or {tier_4_cost} points by tier, and {cost_per_colony} more for every colony beyond the first. Points left over when a technology completes carry into the next one.

The capital produces {capital_research} research in each branch; researchers add more. You can spend {reroll_cost} [[resource:influence|influence]] to redraw a branch's hand. Story technologies, marked with a star, are always offered once you can study them.
