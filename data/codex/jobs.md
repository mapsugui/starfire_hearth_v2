# Jobs

Districts create jobs, and settlers fill them in order: the most productive district first, then the lowest slot. Farmers make [[resource:food|food]], technicians [[resource:energy|energy]], miners [[resource:minerals|minerals]], metallurgists [[resource:alloys|metals]] and researchers [[resource:research|research]]. Clerks in Habitation districts make a little energy and add stability.

Each thousand settlers without a job costs the colony {unemployed} stability. When there are more jobs than settlers, the least productive jobs stay empty.
