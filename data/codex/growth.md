# Settlers and growth

A colony grows towards its next thousand settlers. Each turn it covers {growth_base} of the way, plus {growth_per_free} for every free home, up to {growth_max} a turn. Worlds that are hard to live on grow more slowly ([[mechanic:habitability|habitability]]), and while anyone is homeless growth falls by {homeless_growth}. Growth stops while [[resource:food|food]] runs short or the stock is empty.

A settlement becomes a Colony at {colony_at} settlers and a City at {city_at}. A City has room for {city_extra_buildings} more building.
