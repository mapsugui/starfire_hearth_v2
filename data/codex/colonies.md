# Colonies

A Colony Ship settles a surveyed world in {colonise_turns} turn, landing {colony_pops} settlers and a free first shelter: a Habitation district on an open world, or a [[building:habitat_dome|Habitat Dome]] on a dome world. A colony counts as developed at {developed_pops} settlers and {developed_districts} districts. From then on it [[mechanic:growth|grows]] like any other.
