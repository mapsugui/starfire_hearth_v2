# Governors

A governor runs a colony's [[mechanic:construction|construction]] for you. Each turn it sets aside a share of your minerals income, {governor_budget} unless you change it, in the colony's purse, up to {funds_cap}, then builds what its focus calls for and says why. Veto a plan and the governor will not try it again for {veto_turns} turns. Turning a governor off returns its purse.
