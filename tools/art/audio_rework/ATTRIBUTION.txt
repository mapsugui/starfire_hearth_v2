Starfire Hearth — before/after audio rendition review, 7 October 2026

Original versions: bit-identical copies from assets/delivered/ in the existing
Starfire Hearth project. All original project credits and rights remain applicable.
No original game audio has been overwritten or published by this review process.

New versions: full-length original arrangements/recompositions for the established
cue roles, performed with recorded sample libraries and designed synthesis. They
are not note-for-note transcriptions or EQ-only remasters. Human speech is absent;
Vael cues are nonverbal tonal sound effects. Plucked-harp parts are used for the
weathered/domestic roles; a recorded nylon-guitar library was not used.

The complete upstream SFZ velocity-layered piano was rendered by sfizz. In this
review its recordings were sequenced, transposed as mapped, stereo-positioned,
mixed, reverberated and level-adjusted. SFX use trimmed/transposed piano excerpts
with envelopes and additional designed layers. The original recordings and their
license are unchanged. Credit is included here and in both listening players.

VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner and contributors
https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Snapshot: 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e
CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Recorded strings, horns/trumpet, flute, clarinet, bassoon, harp, glockenspiel,
marimba and percussion. Custom SFZ maps set concert pitch, velocity and
round-robin regions; sustain sample copies received crossfade loops. Rendered
parts were positioned, sequenced, mixed and given a shared hall treatment.
Raw upstream sample files remain unchanged. Selected percussion recordings also
provide short tactile, metallic and drum transients for individually designed SFX.

sfizz 1.2.3 offline rendering engine
https://sfz.tools/sfizz/  https://github.com/sfztools/sfizz
BSD 2-Clause. Offline rendering, not a new runtime dependency of the game.
Intermediate sampler stems are 16-bit PCM; final mixes/masters are 24-bit PCM.
New delivered renditions follow existing OGG/WAV filenames and roles. Browser
copies use AAC/M4A; the original before/ files are retained losslessly as supplied.

Library provenance, sample hashes and modified sample maps remain under
/workspace/.starfire-setup/audio-libraries/. Instrument libraries, executables and
intermediate stems are not included in the listening ZIP. The scripts and musical
scores document the arrangement and processing for subsequent refinement.

Revision 2: note lengths/overlaps and same-pitch ties were revised, piano sustain
pedal controls added, and struck/plucked releases lengthened. Velocity ranges are
now mapped per pitch so sparse sample layers leave no unplayable volume ranges.
Offline sampler maps use full-RAM loading; Salamander Offline.sfz is an additional
wrapper, and the upstream Salamander instrument/sources remain unchanged.
The high metallic music lead is replaced with lower clarinet and soft marimba;
repeating high colour accents now use quieter, lower-register wooden mallets.
The original game audio and first-rendition review remain intact.
